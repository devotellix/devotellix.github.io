Privacy, in plain language

Policy prepared September 8, 2026. Effective when the app is released.

Audio stays in your workflow

Audio is processed on your device. TakeTether uses no account, ads or usage analytics and does not send recordings to a developer server. Projects keep imported copies and aligned results in this app until you delete the project or remove the app; they may be included in device backups. Temporary working copies are cleared on restart. Exported files stay with the location or provider you choose, which may be a cloud service. External websites and email use their own services.

Preferences and Apple services

The app stores an export count and review-request dates in its own preferences. These may be included in your device backup. Apple controls whether a native review prompt appears. The app does not know whether you submit a rating.

Support messages and this website

If you email Devotellix, your email provider and the support mailbox process the information you choose to send. Do not send sensitive recordings unless they are necessary and you choose to do so. Contact us about support correspondence or privacy questions. This static website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process ordinary access logs under its own privacy policy. We add no website analytics or advertising trackers.
