CarLook Studio

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

This policy explains how Devotellix handles information when you use CarLook Studio and this website. CarLook is a paid photo-editing app with no subscription or in-app purchases. No CarLook account registration is required.

1. Photos and AI processing

Before a render is sent, the app explains the transfer and asks you to confirm. Your chosen photo and selected modification instructions are then sent directly from your device to the OpenAI Images API to create the preview. Canceling the confirmation does not send the photo. The app does not upload your entire photo library.

CarLook does not route the request through a Devotellix rendering server or maintain an online photo gallery. The generated image is returned directly to the app. Save the result to Photos if you want to keep it. A result lost during a network interruption cannot be retrieved later from a CarLook server.

A photo can contain information that identifies you or someone else, even when the app does not send a name or account identifier. The photo and edit instructions are processed off your device by OpenAI. Ordinary connection information, including your IP address, is also visible to the provider when your device connects.

OpenAI applies its own API data controls. Its published policy says API data is not used for model training by default unless the customer opts in. Default abuse-monitoring retention can be up to 30 days, with exceptions described in its policy. CarLook does not promise immediate deletion from the provider's systems or zero retention. See OpenAI API data controls.

2. Local monthly allowance

The app permits up to 10 completed renders per UTC calendar month on each device. Its monthly usage counter and request reservations are stored locally in the iOS Keychain. This allowance is not synchronized through iCloud or shared between devices. It is not tied to a CarLook account or an Apple acquisition identifier sent to a developer server.

The counter rolls over at the start of a new UTC calendar month; unused renders do not carry over. A request with an uncertain outcome can keep a place reserved for the current month. Removing the app does not guarantee that the local Keychain allowance record is removed.

Apple handles the one-time app purchase. We do not receive payment card details. CarLook does not offer subscriptions or in-app purchases.

3. Device storage and permissions

The app stores preferences, onboarding progress, and notification settings locally. It does not maintain a developer-hosted history of your photos or renders. Images you save to Photos remain there until you delete them; sharing an image sends it to the destination you choose.

Camera access is optional and needed only to take a photo. The system photo picker lets you choose an existing image. Saving a generated image requires permission to add to Photos. Notification permission is optional. You can change app permissions in iOS Settings.

4. Third parties and tracking

OpenAI processes the photo and instructions needed for your requested image edit. Its handling of processing and security records is described in the API data-controls policy linked above. CarLook does not include advertising, cross-app tracking, or a developer-operated analytics service. We do not sell user photos or use them for targeted advertising.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its typeface from Google Fonts. Visiting it makes ordinary web requests to those services, which can receive connection information such as your IP address. See the GitHub privacy statement and Google privacy policy for those website services.

5. Your choices and contact

You can cancel a render before the photo transfer, revoke camera or photo permissions in Settings, and delete saved images from Photos. There is no CarLook online account to delete. Deleting the app does not delete provider-held processing records or guarantee removal of the local Keychain allowance counter.

For privacy questions or access/deletion requests, contact us. We will explain what information we hold and what is handled by the provider; we cannot promise to retrieve a lost render or erase records we do not control. If you contact support by email, we receive the information you choose to send. Please do not include payment card details, passwords, or private photos in an initial message.
