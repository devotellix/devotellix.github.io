Help with your calculation

Include the app version, iOS version, tool, units and steps needed to reproduce the issue. Replace confidential drawing values with a small example.

How do I get a first result?

Choose a calculator, replace the illustrative inputs with your own values, then tap Calculate. Check the units and expand Method to read assumptions and sources.

Why did a result disappear?

Changing an input invalidates the old result. Tap Calculate again after correcting all fields. Save is available after a valid calculation.

How do I keep a case?

Tap Save, choose an existing project or enter a new project name, and name the case. Reopen it from Projects to edit its inputs. Save before leaving if you want to retain your work.

How do I export or delete?

Open a project, prepare its PDF, then use the system sharing control. Project and case deletion requires confirmation. A deleted record does not delete copies you previously shared.

What are the limits?

RSS relies on the stated independence and distribution assumptions. Thermal calculations use your coefficient across your chosen temperature range. Tap drill is an approximate cutting-tap estimate. Hole/shaft clearance uses your explicit limits, not an ISO designation lookup.

Do calculations need a connection?

No. Bundled methods and calculations work offline. External source, privacy and support pages need a connection. No account or subscription is required.
