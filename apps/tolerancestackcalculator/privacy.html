Privacy and your calculations

The app performs calculations on your device. It has no developer-operated account, cloud sync, advertising or analytics service.

Local data

Project and case names, component labels, numeric inputs, notes, units and timestamps are saved in the app’s local storage. Unit/theme preferences and limited counters used for native review-request eligibility are also stored locally.

Backups, export and external links

Device backups may include app data. Exporting a PDF sends its contents only to the destination you choose in the system share sheet. Opening a source or help link visits an external website under its own privacy practices. No project is sent to the developer automatically. Pinned tool identifiers are also stored locally. Copying coordinates writes only the selected table to the system clipboard after your explicit action.

Retention and deletion

Saved records remain until you delete them in the app or delete the app. Offloading the app is different from deleting it. Previously exported copies and backups must be managed separately. Generated temporary PDFs can remain until iOS clears temporary storage.

Permissions and tracking

The app does not request camera, microphone, photo-library, contacts, location or notification access. It does not track you or use third-party analytics SDKs. Apple’s purchase and review services operate under Apple’s terms.

This website and support

This static site uses no added analytics, advertising, third-party scripts or cookies. GitHub Pages serves the site and may process technical request data under GitHub’s privacy statement. If you email support, the developer and mail services receive the information you voluntarily send. Avoid sending confidential drawings or project data unless necessary.
