PROTEIN SWAP · IPHONE & IPAD

From protein grams to food on your plate.

Enter the protein amount from a serving. Explore weighed food alternatives, adjust the portions and compare the numbers that matter to you.

40 foods. Clear prepared weights. Your own prices.

01

Compare real portions

Find one- or two-food combinations close to your entered protein amount. See protein, energy, carbohydrates and fat together.

02

Make it your combination

Adjust grams, lock a portion and rebalance. Filter by vegetarian or vegan choices, exclude foods and use your available ingredients.

03

Read a label, check the values

Choose a photo for on-device text recognition. Confirm the protein, energy and per-serving or per-100-g basis before applying it.

04

Compare your own costs

Enter package prices and prepared weights. See a portion cost only when the required prices and currencies are compatible.

05

Keep what is useful

Save favorites for later. Record what you ate explicitly, keep dated entries and compare today’s recorded protein with an optional goal you chose.

Designed for iPhone and iPad, iOS 17 or later. Interface and food descriptions in English, French and Spanish. App Store listing link will appear here when available.

A practical comparison tool, not a diet prescription. Nutrient values are reference estimates and vary with products and preparation. Recorded entries do not represent your entire diet. The app does not set your protein needs.
