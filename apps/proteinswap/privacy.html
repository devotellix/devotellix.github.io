PROTEIN SWAP · PRIVACY

Your data stays under your control.

Protein Swap processes comparisons and selected label images on your device. No account is required, and the app includes no advertising or analytics SDK.

What the app stores

Food selections and exclusions, entered prices, favorites, explicitly recorded portions and nutrient snapshots, and an optional personal goal are saved in the app’s protected local storage. A completed-comparison count and review-request dates support a limited native review request.

Photo labels

You select a single photo using Apple’s photo picker. Recognition runs locally using Apple Vision. The app keeps the image preview and recognized text only during the import session; it does not upload them or keep an app-owned photo copy. The system may download an iCloud photo you choose and manage temporary provider files. Your original photo remains in your library.

No tracking or broad permissions

The app does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, HealthKit or full photo-library access. It does not send your entries to a developer-operated server. There is no app cloud synchronization.

Retention, backups and deletion

Local records remain until you remove them. In About, choose “Erase all my data” and confirm to clear the app’s saved data, drafts and undo history. The bundled food catalogue remains. Device backups may include app data according to your Apple settings. Erasing the active app data does not independently delete older system backups, original photos or support emails.

Support and external links

If you email Devotellix, your email provider sends your address, message and attachments to us so we can respond. Please avoid sending health records or unnecessary personal information. Contact us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to applicable obligations. External reference sites and email providers apply their own policies.
