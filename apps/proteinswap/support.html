A clearer answer, one comparison at a time.

How do I start?

Enter the grams of protein shown for the serving you want to compare, then choose Compare. Add energy or a maximum energy value only if useful. The results show prepared food weights, not shopping weights.

Why are there no results?

Check the protein amount, energy ceiling, dietary filters and available-food selection. Relax a constraint or add available foods. A combination may not fit the catalogue’s practical portion limits.

Why is a price unknown?

Enter a package price and its prepared edible weight for every food in the combination. All prices must use the same currency. A price per raw package cannot be treated as a prepared-food price without the appropriate prepared weight.

What if label recognition is wrong?

Edit the values manually. Choose whether the label gives a serving or 100 g, and enter the portion weight for a per-100-g label. Confirm the proposed values before applying them. A photo stored only in iCloud may need a network connection to download first.

Does saving a favorite log a meal?

No. A favorite is reusable. Use Record intake explicitly when you want a dated entry. The daily comparison includes only the entries you recorded and does not estimate missing food.

How do I delete data?

Remove individual favorites or history entries from Saved. History deletion offers Undo. To erase all local data, open About, choose Erase all my data and confirm. This full erase cannot be undone.

Can the app choose a nutrition goal?

No. The optional daily goal is a number you enter yourself. The app compares food reference values and recorded entries; it does not diagnose conditions or determine dietary needs.
