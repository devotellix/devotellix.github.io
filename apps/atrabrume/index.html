Atrabrume

Spend a Halloween evening in a haunted manor on Apple TV. Settle by the fire, follow clues through three doors or choose when a figure will appear.

An evening by the fire

Choose which visitors appear in the pumpkin room and how often they return. For a quiet evening, the autumn orangery and the seaside garden have no sound or apparitions.

The three doors

Read the clue and choose the door with the matching symbol. Collect three key fragments within five rounds.

A timed apparition

Pick the crypt skeleton, the figure at the window or the face in the embers. Set the delay for each appearance, then play the sequence once or on a loop.

Before a scare

Before starting or replaying a scare, tell everyone in the room that a figure will appear after a dark, silent interval, with a sudden noise if sound is on. Ask if they want to take part and start at low volume. Press Back on the remote to pause the sequence.
