Privacy

Using the app

The app works offline and does not collect or share personal data. It has no accounts, ads or tracking tools and does not access the microphone or camera.

Data on your Apple TV

The Apple TV saves your language, intensity, sound and motion settings, room and visitor settings, scheduled scare sequence and best score. To erase these data, select the app on the Apple TV Home Screen, choose Delete App and then Delete. Offload keeps the data.

Visiting this website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit a page, GitHub logs and stores your IP address for security purposes. You can read more in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

GitHub Privacy Statement

Contacting support

If you email support, the recipient receives your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send so they can answer your request. You can use the same support contact for questions about your personal data or to request deletion of your correspondence.

contact.devotellix@gmail.com

Your privacy rights

The support address uses Gmail. If the GDPR applies to you, you may request access, correction, deletion or restriction of your personal data, as applicable. You may also contact your local data protection authority.
