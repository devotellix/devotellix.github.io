Why did the timer stop?

Keep the app open on the Apple TV. Switching to another app pauses the sequence. When you return, choose Resume when everyone is ready. A scheduled start uses the Apple TV’s local time.

How do I make a scare quieter?

In Settings, lower Master volume or Effects and check the TV volume. Start at low volume if you try Sound check. The sample includes a sudden knock on glass.

What should I include in a support request?

Tell us which scene you opened, what you selected and what happened. Include your Apple TV model and tvOS version if you know them. A password or Apple Account details are unnecessary.
