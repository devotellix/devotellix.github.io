A quiet moment, shaped by you.

Listen to sounds inspired by head massage and hair care. Start a short session, choose a texture, or draw a stereo movement and listen to it repeat.

Listen immediately

13 bundled sounds and 6 prepared rituals. Headphones make the left-to-right movement easier to hear.

Keep your own movement

Capture a short sound movement, save it on your device and replay it across textures. Compose and save sessions from your chosen steps.

An optional mirror

A front-camera preview can accompany the touch surface. It is optional, stays on your device and records no photos or video.

For iPhone and iPad, iOS/iPadOS 17 or later. App languages: English, French, German, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese and Japanese. Listening, composing and help work offline.

An audio experience, not a physical massage or medical treatment. Relaxation, sleep and ASMR sensations are not guaranteed. Recordings include adapted source materials; their actual origins are described in the app.
