Help with Champi ASMR

For a problem or a question, email Devotellix. Include the app version and build shown in Settings, your device model, iOS version and what happened. Nothing is attached or sent automatically.

No sound

Check device volume and the audio output. Open sound preferences: a session cannot play if all its sounds are excluded. Try a short session at a comfortable volume.

Mirror unavailable

Listening and touch controls work without camera access. If you want to use the mirror, check the app’s camera permission in iOS Settings. The mirror stops when you leave the studio or the app becomes inactive.

Your saved content

Sessions and your latest saved movement are local to the device. The privacy screen offers an explicit erase action. Backups managed by iOS are separate.

Purchases and billing

App Store purchases, downloads and refund requests are handled through Apple’s services. Devotellix cannot promise an Apple refund.
