Privacy in Champi ASMR

The app has no developer-operated account, advertising, tracking or analytics service. Your sessions and preferences stay in the app’s local storage.

What is stored

Sound preferences, favourites, session names and steps, the latest saved movement and welcome-screen preference are stored on your device. No listening history is sent to Devotellix.

Camera and suggestions

Camera access is requested only when you enable the optional live mirror. The preview is processed on your device; the app does not record photos or video, use the microphone, analyse your face or send camera frames. Name suggestions are standard local suggestions, with no cloud model.

Retention, deletion and backups

Use Settings → Privacy → Erase local data to remove saved app data. You can also delete the app; offloading it is different. iOS backups may contain app data and must be managed separately. Devotellix cannot remotely access your local sessions or device backups.

External websites and email

Opening a source, licence, support or privacy link visits a website under its own privacy practices. GitHub Pages hosts these pages and may process technical request data. These pages add no analytics, advertising scripts or tracking cookies. If you email support, Devotellix and email providers receive what you choose to send.

Questions and requests
