Support for families

An iPhone music drawing workshop for children to use with a grown-up. The interface and spoken instructions are available in English and French.

Privacy

Make a first melody

In the introduction, tap “Try it”. Draw a line and lift your finger to hear it. Draw higher for a higher note. Tap “Replay” to play it again. To revisit the introduction, open “Grown-ups” from the home screen.

Find or delete a drawing

At the end of the introduction, tap “Save my drawing” to keep a page. In “My sketchbook”, the play button replays a melody and the title opens the drawing. To remove a page, tap “Edit”, tap the bin beside the drawing and confirm “Delete”. This deletes the page from this sketchbook. Copies in previous device backups are managed separately.

Listen to the notes

Start at a low volume. If no sound plays, check media volume and the selected headphones or speaker. Restart playback after an interruption. Playing music and spoken instructions does not require microphone access.

An age-range request appears

Depending on your region, Apple Account and iOS version, Apple may ask to share an age range. Follow the iOS prompt. If a required check cannot be completed, review the account settings with a grown-up and try again. The app does not ask for an identity document.

Privacy and device backups

Open the grown-ups’ guide for privacy information. An iPhone backup or transfer may include the sketchbook. Devotellix cannot retrieve it remotely. What iCloud backs up

Contact support

A grown-up can email contact.devotellix@gmail.com. Describe what happened and, if known, the iPhone model, iOS version and app version. A written description is usually sufficient. Please keep your child’s name, photos and artwork on the device. Emailing us is optional.
