Privacy policy

Your iPhone makes music from the lines you draw. Your voice and your drawings are not sent to Devotellix. Ask a grown-up if you have questions.

Support

The sketchbook on your iPhone

The app has no account requirement. Each saved drawing includes a random drawing ID, title, date, points and colours, and settings needed to replay it. A preference remembers whether the introduction was completed or skipped. Spoken instructions use iPhone speech synthesis. The app has no publisher-installed advertising or analytics tools. It does not send your sketchbook to Devotellix.

Keeping and deleting pages

Saved pages stay in the app until you delete them. In “My sketchbook”, tap “Edit”, then the bin next to a drawing, and confirm “Delete”. In iOS, “Delete App” removes local app data and “Offload App” keeps it. Your device backups may include drawings or previous copies. You manage those backups using Apple’s tools. iCloud backups

Apple age range and parental permission

For applicable accounts and regions, the app uses Apple’s age-range service to comply with local child protection requirements. The system may display a choice. When sharing is optional, declining lets you continue. When a check is required and cannot be completed, you can retry. The app uses the signal only for this check, in memory. It does not save your age range, send it to the publisher or ask for a birth date or identity document. Apple manages download permission and its revocation. How Apple’s service works

Emailing the publisher

SAS Devotellix is the controller for enquiries sent to it. Emailing contact.devotellix@gmail.com is optional. The sender’s address, message and any attachments are used to answer the enquiry. Our legitimate interest is to respond to product questions and follow up on issues. Applicable legal obligations require us to respond to privacy rights requests. Recipients are the people handling the request at Devotellix and the email provider, Gmail (Google). A child’s identifying information is not needed to obtain help.

Keeping support correspondence

We keep correspondence for the time needed to reply, check whether the issue is resolved and close the follow-up. After that, material may be retained only as necessary for a legal obligation or for handling a legal claim, for the applicable period. You may request deletion of your correspondence by emailing the address above.

Website visits and international transfers

These static pages use the browser’s internet connection. They have no accounts, forms, advertising, analytics or cookies added by Devotellix. GitHub Pages processes technical data including the visitor’s IP address to route requests and secure its service. The email and hosting providers may process data in the United States and other countries. Their notices explain the destinations and safeguards for relevant transfers, including adequacy decisions and standard contractual clauses. Contact Devotellix if you need further details. Your rights

Depending on the law and processing involved, you can request access, correction, deletion or restriction, and object to processing based on our legitimate interests. Portability applies where its legal conditions are met. A parent or guardian may contact us on behalf of a child. Identify your request without sending the sketchbook or an identity document by default. You can complain to a competent data protection authority, including where you live, work or where the infringement occurred. French CNIL · UK Information Commissioner

Apple handles App Store payment information under its own terms. Devotellix does not receive your card number through the app. Apple privacy policy
