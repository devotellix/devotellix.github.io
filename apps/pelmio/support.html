Pelmio support

For help with a photo, a clip or an export, email Devotellix. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, Pelmio version and the steps you took. A description is usually enough. Please avoid sending private photos or recordings.

Email Devotellix

contact.devotellix@gmail.com

Can I use Pelmio without the camera?

Yes. Open the editor to use a sample scene or choose an image with the system photo picker. To change a permission later, open Settings, then Apps, then Pelmio.

Why is my clip silent?

In Chrono, turn on the microphone before recording and allow access if prompted. If the microphone was off, the clip has no recorded soundtrack.

Where is my photo?

A print saved in Pelmio appears in its contact sheet. To keep a copy in Photos, open the print and choose the option to add it to Photos. You can also use the share sheet to choose another destination.
