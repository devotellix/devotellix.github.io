Privacy in Pelmio

Your photos and clips are processed on your iPhone. Pelmio has no account, advertising, analytics SDK or tracking. The app does not upload your images, recordings or recipes to Devotellix.

Camera, microphone and Photos

Camera access is requested when you activate the camera. Chrono records video with the microphone off by default. If you enable it and allow access, nearby sounds and voices can be recorded. If you decline either permission, you can still edit a sample or import a photo. Pelmio does not request your location.

What stays on the iPhone

Pelmio stores originals, processed prints, clips, recipes and preferences in the app’s local storage. An imported original can already contain location metadata. The system photo picker gives Pelmio access to the image you choose. Photos may download it from iCloud if needed.

Export, sharing and deletion

Adding a finished JPEG to Photos requests add-only access. A shared JPEG does not copy the original’s EXIF or GPS tags. A shared MP4 includes its saved audio. You choose the destination. You can remove prints and clips from Pelmio’s library. Exports and device backups are managed separately, and iCloud Photos may sync exported images according to your settings.

You can review or withdraw camera, microphone and Photos permissions in Settings → Apps → Pelmio. App data is stored locally until you delete it or delete the app. Device backups and previously exported copies are managed at their destinations.

This website and support

GitHub Pages hosts these pages and may process technical request information such as your IP address. These pages add no analytics or advertising. If you email Devotellix, your email address, message and any attachments are handled by Devotellix and its email provider to answer your request. For questions about your correspondence or to request its deletion, use the address below. Devotellix cannot access the photos stored in your app remotely.
